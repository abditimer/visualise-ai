01 / A way in

Make the invisible
easier to see.

AI can feel like a black box: all output, no view of the moving parts. This collection starts with a simple idea—give difficult things a shape, a sequence, and room to ask questions.

Follow a visual lesson, wander through a reference, or get closer to the systems themselves. Each project is an independent window into the same changing landscape.
