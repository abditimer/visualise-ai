What shapes the cost?

Explore a simplified retirement-income calculation. Higher discount rates reduce the present value; a longer payment horizon increases it.

Illustrative present value

Toy calculation: sum annual income ÷ (1 + discount rate)^year over the selected horizon. Fixed annual payments, no mortality, fees or inflation. This is an actuarial illustration, not a trained ML model. Original company work belongs to Legal & General; this demo is independently authored.

Independent illustration · synthetic inputs

How far does the curve reach?

Move the synthetic survival horizon to see how the curve and expected years of payments change.

Expected payment-years within 40 years

Synthetic survival S(t) = exp(−ln(2) × (t / median)^2). Expected payment-years = sum of S(t) at years 1–40. The median marks 50% survival; it is not the mean. No real demographic data or company longevity model is used. Original work belongs to Legal & General.

Simulation only; Spark does not run in this browser. Assumed single-worker time = rows × 4 seconds. Parallel time = serial time × (0.15 + 0.85 / workers) + 0.8 × (workers − 1) seconds. Balanced partitions and a fixed 15% serial component; actual performance depends on joins, shuffle, skew, storage and cluster configuration. PhD · University of Birmingham · ongoing

What does a model retain?

Nudging studies how the amount of text shown to a language model affects its ability to reproduce what follows.

Reveal a prefixGenerateMeasure recovery

The study compares texts with independently verified training membership against non-member controls, using a fixed continuation target. The aim is to understand when recovery exceeds what predictability alone might explain.

It sits within my broader interest in mechanistic interpretability and AI safety. The work is still in progress; I’m not presenting a completed result here.

Recovery is behavioural evidence consistent with exposure, not proof of training membership. The research repository remains private. Public methods and results will follow as the work develops.
